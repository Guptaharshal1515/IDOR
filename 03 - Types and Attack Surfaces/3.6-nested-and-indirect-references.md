# 3.6 Nested and Indirect References

Nested resources express relationships in the URL or body:

```text
/teams/10/projects/42/issues/8
/accounts/7/messages/91
```

A common mistake is validating only one relationship. The server may confirm that User A belongs to Team 10 but fail to verify that Project 42 belongs to Team 10, or that Issue 8 belongs to Project 42.

Indirect references include job IDs, invitation IDs, reset records, share tokens, and object handles returned by another endpoint. They may not look like database IDs, but they still identify resources. Authorize after resolving the reference and before returning or changing the object.
