# 3.7 Multi-Tenant IDOR

In a multi-tenant application, several organizations share a service while their data should remain isolated. If a database query trusts a client-supplied tenant ID, one customer may access another customer’s records.

```python
# Unsafe when tenant_id comes from the request.
records = Record.query.filter_by(tenant_id=request.args["tenant_id"]).all()
```

The tenant should normally be derived from the authenticated session and policy context, then applied server-side. Support or delegated-access exceptions should be explicit, limited, logged, and tested.

Cross-tenant IDORs often have wider impact because one account may reach many records belonging to another organization. Use two approved tenants and non-sensitive records, including exports, search, bulk actions, and jobs.
